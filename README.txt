TradeAssist working prototype

Open TradeAssist-dashboard.html in Chrome or Edge.

What is tied in now:
- Dashboard cards open Leads, Quotes, and Invoices.
- Add Lead saves a lead and creates/updates customer history.
- Lead Call/Quote/Remove buttons update the app.
- Quotes can be approved, followed up, or turned into invoices.
- Invoices can be nudged or marked paid.
- Bookings can be added, confirmed, and reminders marked sent.
- Message templates can be added and send counts update.
- Follow-ups can be added and completed.
- Reports calculate from saved records.
- Settings save business info and theme color.
- Everything persists in browser local storage.
